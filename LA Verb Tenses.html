VERB TENSES

A verb tense shows the time an action happens. There are three main verb tenses:

  • Present Tense – action happening now or regularly.
  • Past Tense – action that happened before now.
  • Future Tense – action that will happen later.

Each of these tenses can be divided into four forms:

  1. Simple Tense
  2. Perfect Tense
  3. Continuous Tense
  4. Perfect Continuous Tense

PRESENT TENSES

1. Simple Present Tense

Structure: S + V + O

  1. I read every day.
    S=I | V=read | O=every day

  2. She washes her clothes.
    S=She | V=washes | O=her clothes

  3. They play football.
    S=They | V=play | O=football

  4. John goes to school.
    S=John | V=goes | O=to school

  5. We eat rice daily.
    S=We | V=eat | O=rice daily

  6. The sun rises daily.
    S=The sun | V=rises | O=daily

  7. He drinks water.
    S=He | V=drinks | O=water

  8. The baby cries loudly.
    S=The baby | V=cries | O=loudly

  9. Fatmata studies hard.
    S=Fatmata | V=studies | O=hard

  10. Birds fly in the sky.
    S=Birds | V=fly | O=in the sky

2. Present Perfect Tense

Structure: S + has/have + V + O

  1. I have eaten breakfast.
    S=I | have | V=eaten | O=breakfast

  2. She has written a letter.
    S=She | has | V=written | O=a letter

  3. They have visited Bo.
    S=They | have | V=visited | O=Bo

  4. We have finished work.
    S=We | have | V=finished | O=work

  5. He has cleaned the room.
    S=He | has | V=cleaned | O=the room

  6. You have seen the movie.
    S=You | have | V=seen | O=the movie

  7. Musa has arrived.
    S=Musa | has | V=arrived | O=—

  8. The teacher has marked books.
    S=Teacher | has | V=marked | O=books

  9. The pupils have gone home.
    S=Pupils | have | V=gone | O=home

  10. I have lost my pen.
    S=I | have | V=lost | O=my pen

3. Present Continuous Tense

Structure: S + am/is/are + V-ing + O

  1. I am studying English.
    S=I | am | V-ing=studying | O=English

  2. She is cooking food.
    S=She | is | V-ing=cooking | O=food

  3. They are playing football.
    S=They | are | V-ing=playing | O=football

  4. We are singing songs.
    S=We | are | V-ing=singing | O=songs

  5. He is writing a letter.
    S=He | is | V-ing=writing | O=a letter

  6. The baby is sleeping.

    S=You | are | V-ing=learning | O=grammar

  8. The boys are running.
    S=Boys | are | V-ing=running | O=—

  9. Fatmata is sweeping room.
    S=Fatmata | is | V-ing=sweeping | O=room

  10. The teacher is teaching.
    S=Teacher | is | V-ing=teaching | O=—
