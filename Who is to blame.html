PROSE – “WHO IS TO BLAME” (Alhaji Mohamed Sesay)

Alhaji Mohamed Sesay, the author of the novel “Who is To Blame”, is a Sierra Leonean and a teacher by profession. His book “Who is To Blame” is a fictional story about the main character, Sento Kanu, who was the only daughter of peasant parents; Mr Alie Kanu and his wife, Yabome Kanu.

Sento was born in a remote village called Maramu. She was raised partly in a village, a town, and the city. She was a slim, black and beautiful girl with a very special character that attracted many people. She was also intelligent, decent, and quick in her movements. These qualities were supported by her good performance in school, home duties, and social life. Sento was very aware of these qualities, so she became proud and forgot her poor background, which later led to her sad ending.

The novel begins by explaining Sento’s family background. She was the only daughter in a family of three children; her two elder brothers were living on their own. As a child, she helped her poor parents in farm work, including upland farming and a large plantain farm with mixed crops. The main source of income for the family was from Mr. Alie Kanu’s farming, animal rearing, and fish caught using traps.

Despite being a hardworking station manager in Bradford, Mr. Jones was also a good night hunter and hardly returned home without game. He shared his catch with neighbours and sold some. Because of his kind nature, he was loved by everyone in Bradford and beyond.

During an initiation ceremony of fifteen young girls in Maramu, Mr. Jones was invited. It was there he saw Sento and became interested in her, thinking she would become successful if well taken care of. After some disagreement about Sento’s adoption, the Kanu family agreed, and Sento went to live with the Jones family in Bradford. Later, they all moved to Freetown when Mr. Jones was transferred to Cline Town Railway Station as manager.

In Bradford, Sento attended UMC Primary School where Mrs. Jones taught. She was very brilliant and loved by her teachers and head teacher. She passed the Selective Entrance Examination successfully and went to Freetown Secondary School for Girls (FSSG). There she continued to show her intelligence.

After admission, the Jones family organized a party for her. At age 16, her parents asked for her return so she could be initiated into the Bondo society according to tradition. The Jones family agreed, and she went back to Maramu where she was initiated with twelve other girls. After two weeks of training, she graduated into womanhood in a ceremony attended by both families.

Mr. Jones later transferred to Freetown and asked the Kanu family to bring Sento to continue her education. Although Yabome Kanu disagreed at first, she later accepted. Sento moved to Freetown with the Jones family.

At Freetown, Sento continued to perform very well in school and behaved well at home. She was always top of her class. During a prize-giving ceremony, Alhaji Kassim, a rich businessman, noticed her and gave her a gift. From that time, he became interested in her and started visiting the Jones family, often bringing gifts like rice, onions, sugar, potatoes, and salt.

After Sento’s GCE A’Level exams, she achieved one of the best results in the country. Alhaji Kassim supported the Jones family to organize a big party for her. Later, he declared his intention to marry her. Sento refused because she wanted to continue her education. However, Kassim insisted and influenced Mr. Jones with gifts and pressure, and eventually Sento was forced into marriage.

After some time, Kassim married another wife, Amie, from Masimra. Sento did not like Amie and they often argued because she felt superior to her.

Later, Kassim fulfilled his promise and married Sento traditionally. Many important people attended the wedding, including chiefs, religious leaders, politicians, and both families. The wedding took place at Hamdala Mosque, Naimbana Street, and the reception was held at YWCA Hall, Brookfields.

During the celebration, Mr. Alie Kanu became drunk and misbehaved because he could not use the modern toilet. He became embarrassed and returned to his village without explanation. When Sento learned about her father’s shame, she ran away and stayed with her friend Musu Marrah.

Later, Sento and Musu decided to travel to Guinea by boat, but there was a storm and the boat sank. Sento and Musu lost their lives.

After searching for them, Mr. Jones and Alhaji Kassim later discovered that they were among those who died in the accident. Their bodies were recovered and buried at Murray Town Cemetery.

Characters in the Novel - Who is to Blame?

CHARACTERS IN THE NOVEL: WHO IS TO BLAME?

Main Characters

  • Sento Kanu: Daughter of Mr. Alie Kanu and Mrs. Yabome Kanu; foster child of the Jones family; later married Alhaji Kassim.
  • Mr. Alie Kanu: Sento’s father and husband of Mrs. Yabome Kanu.
  • Mrs. Yabome Kanu: Sento’s mother and wife of Mr. Alie Kanu.
  • Amadu Kanu: Sento’s eldest brother.
  • Karimu Kanu: Sento’s older brother, known as a womaniser.

The Kanu Family and Related Characters

  • Ya Fathu: Aunt to Mr. Alie Kanu; she informed Alhaji Kassim and Mr. Jones that the family had gone to the farm.
  • Pa Morlai: A blacksmith and fortune teller consulted about Sento’s whereabouts.
  • Foday: Apprentice to the blacksmith who called Mr. and Mrs. Kanu from the farm.
  • Borbor Sankoh: Sento’s houseboy who assisted in family duties and helped Mr. Alie Kanu when drunk.

The Jones Family (Foster Family)

  • Mr. Henry Jones: Railway station manager in Bradford; foster father of Sento.
  • Mrs. Jones: Teacher and foster mother of Sento.
  • Mabel Jones: Daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Jones.

Community Leaders and Authorities

  • Pa Sembu: Village headman of Maramu.
  • Mr. Santigie Kanu: Section chief involved in Sento’s adoption meeting.
  • Sheik Ibrahim Conteh: Leader of the delegation for Sento’s customary marriage.
  • Pa Abdul Rahman Cole: Friend of Mr. Jones and involved in Sento’s first marriage arrangements.

Alhaji Kassim’s Family and Associates

  • Alhaji Kassim: Prominent businessman in Freetown; husband of Sento Kanu and Amie Turay.
  • Amie Turay: Second wife of Alhaji Kassim.
  • Mr. Amidu Turay: Father of Amie Turay.
  • Haja Memuna Kassim: Late wife of Alhaji Kassim.
  • Pa Umaru: Leader of a delegation pleading for Kassim’s marriage arrangement.
  • Mr. Thomas Edwards: Friend of Kassim who never refuses requests.

Friends and Other Characters

  • Musu Marrah: Sento’s classmate at FSSG and close friend.
